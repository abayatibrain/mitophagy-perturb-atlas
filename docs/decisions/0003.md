# ADR-0003: Replogle 2022 cell-line subset
Date: 2026-05-19
Status: Proposed — awaiting Armin sign-off

## Context

Replogle 2022 (*Cell*) released genome-wide CRISPRi Perturb-Seq in
two cell lines: K562 (chronic myeloid leukemia, hematopoietic) and
RPE1 (retinal pigment epithelium, non-cancerous). The two lines
have systematically different mitophagy biology — RPE1 has more
mitochondrial mass per cell and a slower turnover; K562 is a fast-
dividing cancer line with elevated baseline lactate production.

For a PRKN/PINK1-mitophagy atlas, choosing which subset to use
shapes which phenocopy candidates surface. The decision needs to be
made up front and defended.

## Options considered

### Option A — K562 only
Cancer hematopoietic line, the more deeply sampled of the two in
Replogle.
- Pros: larger N per perturbation; lower technical noise; matches
  the cell-type context of DepMap's heaviest representation.
- Cons: cancer-context confound for any neurodegen-relevant claim;
  hematopoietic mitochondrial biology differs from neuronal.

### Option B — RPE1 only
Non-cancerous epithelial line.
- Pros: less cancer-context confound; closer baseline mitochondrial
  state to non-dividing post-mitotic cells.
- Cons: smaller N per perturbation; not in DepMap's deepest
  coverage, so the side-by-side comparison from ADR-0001 is less
  symmetric.

### Option C — Both, kept in parallel columns
Replogle K562 in one similarity table, Replogle RPE1 in another;
phenocopy candidates that converge across both get a confidence
badge.
- Pros: convergence across cell lines is itself a signal; matches
  the spirit of ADR-0001 (separate spaces, side-by-side display);
  doesn't throw away half the public data.
- Cons: three similarity tables to maintain (DepMap + K562 +
  RPE1) instead of two.

## Criteria for choosing
1. **Match to ADR-0001's framing.** That ADR keeps DepMap and
   Perturb-Seq as separate spaces. Keeping K562 and RPE1 as
   separate sub-spaces is consistent.
2. **Statistical power per perturbation.** Both subsets have
   genome-wide coverage; the question is whether the additional
   table is worth the engineering cost.
3. **Honest reporting on the cancer confound.** Both Replogle
   subsets are immortalised cell lines, but K562's leukemic origin
   adds a stronger interpretive caveat for neurodegeneration
   relevance.

## Decision

**Option C: both K562 and RPE1, kept in parallel columns alongside
DepMap.** A phenocopy candidate that scores high in all three gets
a "convergent across modalities and lines" badge in the dossier.
Candidates that score high in only one line get the per-line label
explicitly, so a reviewer can see which cell-context produced the
signal.

## Consequences
- Three similarity tables maintained: DepMap, Replogle-K562,
  Replogle-RPE1.
- The empirical-null FDR (ADR-0006) is computed independently for
  each of the three.
- The README must explicitly call out the cancer-context confound
  for K562 and the smaller-N caveat for RPE1.

## Open questions for Armin
1. Confirm Option C (both subsets), or override to a single subset
   for the v1.0.0 release.
2. Convergence badge threshold: should "convergent" require all
   three modalities, or two of three?
